# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in AutoRepo.ai to be respectful, constructive, and safe
for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment,
discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are
not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Make room for differing perspectives and ask for clarification in good faith.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and security boundaries.
- Give actionable, proportionate feedback.
- Follow repository governance and clean-room requirements.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Threats, harassment, hate speech, sexualized content, or unwanted contact.
- Publishing private information, security-sensitive details, or source-restricted
  material without authorization.
- Bad-faith reporting, impersonation, manipulation of project evidence, or
  attempts to evade review controls.
- Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

The O1 program/release authority administers this code of conduct and may ask
for correction, remove content, limit participation, or take other
proportionate action. Security-sensitive reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md),
not public discussion. GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting is active and
read-back verified; use the private route for vulnerability details.

O1 actions are recorded as governed Git evidence when the work ledger is
available. Concerns involving an authority holder should be handled by an
independent human maintainer when one is available; an automation agent cannot
decide an enforcement appeal.
